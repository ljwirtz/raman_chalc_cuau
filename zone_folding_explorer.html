ZnSe and ZnS turned into CuInSe₂ and CuInS₂ in the chalcopyrite (CH) or CuAu (CA) cell. Each slider moves one ingredient (Raman tensors, masses, force constants) from the parent value (0) to the value of the ternary compound (1), so you can follow how the Raman spectrum of the parent turns into that of the ternary compound and which ingredient lights up the zone-folded lines. Data: HSE06 phonons and Raman tensors (FHI-aims + phonopy), with the parent computed in the same CA (4-atom) and CH (8-atom) cells; Supplemental Material to “Zone-folding origin of the CuAu/chalcopyrite Raman splitting in zinc-blende- and wurtzite-derived ternary semiconductors”.

Phonon dispersion: ZnSe (ZB) folded into CH and CA

The Mass and Dynamical matrix sliders above move the CH and CA panels away from ZnSe; the ZB panel stays fixed. All three are plotted along the same path of the ZB zone. A dot is one band of the CH or CA cell at that ZB wavevector; its size and opacity are its ZB character, the weight on the six ZB plane waves (cation and anion, x y z). Grey lines are the ZnSe bands. At 0 the ZB-like bands sit on the grey lines and the rest are folded copies; as the sliders move, weight leaks from the ZB bands into the folded ones, and bands that were degenerate split.

Model: nearest-neighbor Keating force field with Bloch phases (the same force constants as the semiempirical engine), so the dispersion is always the semiempirical one whichever engine is selected above; the ab initio data (HSE06, CRYSTAL, Quantum ESPRESSO) are Γ-only, and are drawn as hollow circles at Γ for comparison. No LO–TO splitting, so the ZB optical branch is flat at Γ. Folded points: CA Γ ← Γ, X(z); CH Γ ← Γ, X(z), W(1,0,±½), W(0,1,±½).

HSE06 (FHI-aims) data and limits

Source: HSE06 (ω = 0.20 Å⁻¹, α = 0.25) calculations with FHI-aims and phonopy, converted to Quantum ESPRESSO .dyn files: ZnSe_CH.dyn and CuInSe2_CH.dyn (8-atom chalcopyrite cell), ZnSe_Au.dyn and CuInSe2_Au.dyn (4-atom CuAu cell). The ZnSe end is computed directly in each supercell, so nothing is folded or rescaled. Each file gives the Γ force constants (Ry/bohr²), masses, positions and the dχ/du Raman tensors (finite differences of ε, Δ = 0.01 Å). The page runs the dynmat.x pipeline: acoustic sum rule, mass-weighted diagonalization, activity 45α² + 7β², Bose factor at 300 K and Lorentzians as in make_spectrum_om.f90. At all sliders = 1 (and 0) the frequencies agree with dynmat.x to 0.005 cm⁻¹ and the Raman activities to a few parts per million (check table above). With the ZnSe tensor, masses and forces, 100 % of the Raman weight sits in the ZnSe TO at 206.2 cm⁻¹ in both cells.

Interpolation: masses (Zn → Cu on the Cu site, Zn → In on the In site), the full force-constant matrix and all dχ/du tensors mix linearly between the ZnSe file (0) and the CuInSe₂ file (1). The atom order and Cartesian frame are the same in both files of a cell. Relaxation (u, c/a, bond lengths) is contained in the force constants, so the dynamical-matrix slider also moves the structure in the ball-and-stick view. The path is not a physical trajectory.

Caveats: ε∞ in the .dyn files is experimental (8.6/8.7 for CuInSe₂, the CH value reused for CA; 5.75 for ZnSe) but enters only the LO–TO term, which is not used at q = 0, so the spectra do not depend on it. The k-point meshes of the dielectric calculations are not recorded in the files. No LO–TO splitting, no resonance. The Raman tensors are not forced to obey the acoustic sum rule unless you tick the box.

HSE06 (FHI-aims) data and limits, sulfides

Source: HSE06 (ω = 0.20 Å⁻¹, α = 0.25) calculations with FHI-aims and phonopy, converted to Quantum ESPRESSO .dyn files: ZnS_CH.dyn and CuInS2_CH.dyn (8-atom chalcopyrite cell), ZnS_Au.dyn and CuInS2_Au.dyn (4-atom CuAu cell). As for the selenides, ZnS is computed directly in each supercell (ideal zinc blende, a₀ = 5.419 Å, not relaxed in the supercell), with the same atom order as the CuInS₂ file of that cell. The page runs the same dynmat.x pipeline: acoustic sum rule, mass-weighted diagonalization, activity 45α² + 7β², Bose factor at 300 K, Lorentzians. At all sliders = 1 (and 0) the frequencies agree with dynmat.x to 0.005 cm⁻¹ and the Raman activities to a few parts per million (check table above). With the ZnS tensor, masses and forces, 100 % of the Raman weight sits in the ZnS TO at 275.7 cm⁻¹ (CH cell) or 275.5 cm⁻¹ (CA cell).

The zone-edge ZnS levels of the two cells differ by up to 0.4 cm⁻¹ (for example 309.4 against 309.8 cm⁻¹), which reflects the different range of the force constants in the two phonopy supercells.

Interpolation: masses (Zn → Cu on the Cu site, Zn → In on the In site), the full force-constant matrix and all dχ/du tensors mix linearly between the ZnS file (0) and the CuInS₂ file (1). Relaxation is contained in the force constants, so the dynamical-matrix slider also moves the structure in the ball-and-stick view. The path is not a physical trajectory.

Caveats: ε∞ in the .dyn files (6.64 for CuInS₂ in both cells, 5.1 for ZnS) enters only the LO–TO term, which is not used at q = 0. No LO–TO splitting, no resonance. The phonon-dispersion section is a ZnSe Keating model and is hidden for the sulfides.
